## Description
Civics 128 is a free, offline flashcard app for the U.S. naturalization civics test used in 2026. No ads, no accounts, no tracking, and nothing to buy.

It contains all 128 official questions and every acceptable answer, reproduced word for word from the official USCIS study materials (published as the 2025 version, which applies to Form N-400 applications filed on or after October 20, 2025).

FEATURES
- All 128 official questions with every acceptable answer
- Home screen showing your progress in each of the eight sections
- Swipe between cards, tap to flip
- Shuffle to study in random order
- Mark cards as known or flag them for review, saved on your device
- The 20 questions in the 65/20 set, for applicants 65 or older with 20 or more years as permanent residents, are badged on the cards
- The official USCIS document is bundled in the app: open it at the exact page for any question, even with no internet
- Light and Dark themes
- Works completely offline

ABOUT THE TEST
At the interview, a USCIS officer asks 20 of the 128 questions; answering 12 correctly passes the civics test. Answers to questions 30, 38, 39, and 57 change with elections and appointments, so the app links straight to the official update page to confirm them before your interview.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no data collection. Your study progress never leaves your device.

Civics 128 is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to USCIS or any government agency.
