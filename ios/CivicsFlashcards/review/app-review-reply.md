3. APP FUNCTIONS AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Civics 128 is a free, offline flashcard study aid for the 2025 U.S. naturalization civics test. It contains all 128 official civics questions and their acceptable answers, reproduced verbatim from the U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) publication "128 Civics Questions and Answers (2025 version)". Users can flip cards to reveal answers, swipe between cards, mark cards as known or flag them for review (progress is stored only on the device), filter by section, shuffle the deck, and verify any answer at the official USCIS source with one tap.

Target audience: adults preparing for the naturalization interview and civics test, i.e. lawful permanent residents who file Form N-400 on or after October 20, 2025 (the date the 2025 test applies from), as well as tutors, family members, and citizenship-class instructors helping them prepare.

Problem solved / value: the official material is a static PDF; the app turns it into a structured study tool with spaced review, progress tracking, and offline access, while keeping the wording exactly as published and linking to the official source so users can confirm answers that change over time (e.g., names of current officials).

4. SETUP AND ACCESS INSTRUCTIONS
No setup is required. Launch the app; the first question card appears immediately. No login, credentials, sample files, or configuration are needed. Tap a card to flip it; swipe left or right (or use the Back/Next buttons) to change cards; use the filter button (top-left) to choose a section, hide known cards, or shuffle; use the info button (top-right) to open the About screen with the data-source links and disclaimer.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS, AND PLATFORMS
None. The app uses no data providers, authentication services, payment processors, analytics, advertising, or AI services, and no third-party SDKs. All content is bundled with the app, and the app makes no network requests of its own. The only network activity occurs if the user taps an outbound link, which opens the official USCIS website (uscis.gov) in the system browser.

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app functions identically in all regions, with identical content and features. The content itself concerns the United States naturalization test and is in U.S. English.
