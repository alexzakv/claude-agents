Civics 128 — Support

What is Civics 128?

A free flashcard app for the 2025 U.S. naturalization civics test — all 128 official USCIS questions and answers, for N-400 applications filed on or after October 20, 2025. Swipe between cards, tap to flip, and track which questions you know. Works fully offline.

Common questions

  • My progress disappeared. Progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes progress; it does not sync between devices.
  • An answer looks different from what I heard. The app reproduces the official USCIS wording verbatim. Answers to questions 24, 30, 38, 39, 53, and 57 change with elections and appointments — verify them at uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates before your interview.
  • Where do the questions come from? The official USCIS document: 128 Civics Questions and Answers (2025 version).
